# Features

The application incorporates functionalities from CareSuite-Web and Well-Being-Hub.

## Core Tracking Features
1. **Mood Tracker**: Select daily moods, write detailed notes, and view analytical mood history chronologically.
2. **Personal Journal**: Write titled journal entries, tag them, and retrieve comprehensive historical journals.
3. **Fitness Tracking**: Log specific physical exercises including workout types, durations, and intensity. Track historical steps and calories.

## Curated Therapy Features
1. **Audio Therapy**: A hub for streaming curated audio content such as peaceful music playlists, mood-focused podcasts, and engaging audiobooks.
2. **Yoga Therapy**: Step-by-step instructions accompanied by videos for therapeutic yoga poses.
3. **Laughing Therapy**: Access to a feed of memes and humorous videos specifically chosen for mood enhancement.
4. **Educational Resources**: Content pages spanning topics of hygiene, general science, and creative arts/crafts.
5. **Mindfulness Game**: An interactive positive-thinking maze game.
6. **AI Chatbot**: An interactive AI-powered conversational agent providing responsive well-being guidance and support.
